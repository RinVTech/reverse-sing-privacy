Privacy Policy for Reverse Sing

Last updated September 21, 2026

RinVTech ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what the Reverse Sing mobile application (the "App") records, what it keeps, and what the ads in it do.

Reverse Sing is designed to operate locally on your device. There is no account to create, and we operate no servers that store your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Microphone access and audio data. The App requires access to your device's microphone to record audio for gameplay. Audio is recorded only while you are recording a take, after you press a record button and grant iOS microphone permission. The App does not listen at any other time, and you can refuse permission and still use the rest of the App. All recorded audio is processed and stored entirely on your device. Your recordings are not uploaded: the App contains no networking code of its own and sends your audio nowhere, so we never receive it.

On-device storage. The App stores game rounds locally in its Application Support folder. This includes audio files, a small manifest file containing the round's name, the date you created it, and the timing of the parts you placed, and a simple flag recording that you have seen the how-to-play screen. Because these are standard iOS application files, they may be included in your device backups, which Apple handles under its own terms and privacy policy.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Reverse Sing is a free application supported by advertisements. We use Google AdMob as our sole third-party service provider to deliver ads. The App does not integrate any third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

  • Advertising data. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect data such as device identifiers, advertising IDs, approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads.
  • User consent and tracking. We comply with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. Ads are personalized only if you grant tracking permission. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we use Google's User Messaging Platform to gather your consent for personalized ads. If you decline tracking or consent, the App serves non-personalized ads instead, and gameplay remains exactly the same. Ads in Reverse Sing are always muted, so they cannot interrupt a round.
  • SKAdNetwork. The App supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, which lets an ad click be credited without identifying you.

For more information on how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Data Sharing

Any sharing of your recordings is entirely at your discretion. When you choose to share a round, the App generates compressed copies of the audio, names them after the round, and passes them to the standard iOS share sheet. The files go only to the destination you choose, and are then governed by the privacy policy of the platform or service you share them to.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not store your data on external servers, you have full control over your information directly on your device:

  • Local deletion. You can open, rename, or delete individual rounds within the App at any time. Deleting a round permanently removes its manifest and all of its audio from your device.
  • App deletion. Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored. We hold no copies of your data.

Because we hold no data about you, this is also how requests to access or delete your information are met.

5. Children's Privacy

Reverse Sing is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Your Privacy Rights and Controls

You can manage your privacy preferences at any time directly through your iOS device:

  • Microphone access. Revoke or grant microphone permission in Settings, Privacy and Security, Microphone.
  • App tracking. Change your tracking answer in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Ad consent (EEA and UK). Reopen the consent form with the Privacy options button above the list of rounds in the App, which appears where your region requires it.

7. Security

Your rounds are stored in the App's own protected directory on your device, relying on Apple's standard iOS security architecture. We run no servers and store none of your data, so there is no collection of it on our side to be breached.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date at the top.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@rinvtech.com.
