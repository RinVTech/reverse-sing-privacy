Privacy Policy for Reverse Sing

Last updated September 21, 2026

Overview

Reverse Sing is a party game for two people and one iPhone, made by RinVTech. It is free and supported by ads. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no server of ours: the game runs on your device and everything you record stays there. This policy explains what the app records, what it keeps, and what the ads in it do.

Microphone and recordings

The app records audio only while you are recording a take, after you press a record button and grant iOS microphone permission. It does not listen at any other time, and you can refuse permission and still use the rest of the app.

Your recordings are not uploaded. We never receive them, and we could not: the app contains no networking code of its own and sends your audio nowhere.

What the app stores on your device

Each round is saved in the app's own Application Support folder as audio files plus a small manifest file, which holds the round's name, the date you created it, and the timing of the parts you placed. The only other thing the app stores is a single on-device flag remembering that you have seen the how-to-play screen. Because these are ordinary app files, they may be included in your device backups, which Apple handles under its own terms.

Sharing

Sharing is optional. When you share a round, the app makes compressed copies of the audio, names them after the round, and passes them to the standard iOS share sheet, where you choose the destination. The copies go only where you send them, and whatever you send them to handles them under its own privacy policy.

Ads, consent, and tracking

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which is the only third-party software in the app. There is no analytics or crash-reporting service in it. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect data such as device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads.

Before the first ad is requested, iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking there. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform decides whether personal data may be used for ads, and you can reopen that form at any time with the Privacy options button above the list of rounds, which appears where your region requires it.

Declining tracking or consent does not remove the ads. The app simply requests non-personalized ones instead, and the game plays exactly the same. Ads in Reverse Sing are always muted so they cannot interrupt a round. The app also supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, which lets an ad click be credited without identifying you.

How long information is kept

Your rounds stay on your device until you remove them. Deleting a round deletes its manifest and all of its audio, and deleting the app removes everything it stored. We hold no copy of any of it.

Children

Reverse Sing is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can look into it.

Your choices and rights

  • Your recordings: open, rename, or delete any round in the app, or delete the app to remove them all. Because we hold no data about you, this is also how requests to access or delete your information are met.
  • Microphone: turn access off at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Microphone.
  • Tracking: change your answer in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Ad consent: reopen the consent form with Privacy options in the app, where it is shown.

Security

Your rounds live in the app's own protected storage on your device, covered by the device's security. We run no servers and store none of your data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
